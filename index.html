What is NYRA Music Player?

NYRA Music Player is a free music streaming web app created by Sahal Shihabudheen, a junior software developer and digital creator from Malappuram, Kerala, India. NYRA features beat-reactive neon visualizers, an AI Mood DJ, a 3D Music Universe, synchronized lyrics, offline playback, and custom playlists — all completely free with no ads.

  • ✅ 100% Free — no ads, no subscription
  • 🎵 Millions of songs to stream instantly
  • 🌈 Beat-reactive neon visualizers
  • 🤖 AI Mood DJ — music that matches your feelings
  • 🌌 3D interactive Music Universe
  • 📝 Synchronized lyrics display
  • 📱 Works on Android, iOS, Desktop — installable PWA
  • 🎧 Offline music listening
  • 🎶 Built-in Spotify playlists (Today's Top Hits, RapCaviar, Top 50 Global & more)
